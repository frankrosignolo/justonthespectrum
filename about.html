About Just on the Spectrum

A child-led, family-centered microschool for home-educating families.

Our model

Just on the Spectrum is not a traditional school or drop-off program. Our child-led, family-centered model is designed for home-educating families who believe parents are a child's most important teacher and should remain actively involved as their child's primary educator and dedicated 1:1 support person throughout the learning journey.

Flexibility over schedule

Because our families are home-educating, flexibility is key. We do not replicate a traditional school schedule, attendance requirements, or educational model. Instead, the learning journey is shaped around each child and family.

What learning can look like

Learning opportunities may include:

  • Community experiences
  • Life skills
  • Communication development
  • Special interests
  • Academics
  • Recreation
  • Parent coaching

Families seeking a traditional school experience with daily attendance, teacher-directed instruction, and responsibility transferred to staff may not find this program to be the right fit.

Enrollment

Enrollment is limited and based on program fit, family alignment, safety considerations, and available space. The questionnaire is the first step — it helps us understand your family and whether our model is a good match.
