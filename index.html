Family Interest & Enrollment Questionnaire

Thank you for your interest in Just on the Spectrum Microschool.

Just on the Spectrum is not a traditional school or drop-off program. Our child-led, family-centered model is designed for home-educating families who believe parents are a child's most important teacher and should remain actively involved as their child's primary educator and dedicated 1:1 support person throughout the learning journey.

Because our families are home-educating, flexibility is key. We do not replicate a traditional school schedule, attendance requirements, or educational model. Learning opportunities may include community experiences, life skills, communication development, special interests, academics, recreation, and parent coaching.

Families seeking a traditional school experience with daily attendance, teacher-directed instruction, and responsibility transferred to staff may not find this program to be the right fit.

Enrollment is limited and based on program fit, family alignment, safety considerations, and available space.
