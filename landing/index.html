Our story

No experts in suits. Just parents in the thick of it, sharing what actually helps.

Just on the Spectrum started with a camera, a kitchen table, and a kid who sees the world differently. We post the moments most families keep private — the waiting rooms, the victories, the 2 a.m. worries — because somewhere out there, another parent is living the same moment and wondering if they're the only one.

Along the way, the diary became a library: step-by-step guides, printable tools, and honest talk about therapies, school, and self-care. Everything we make answers one question — what do I wish someone had told us sooner?

What we make

Four ways we've got your back

01

Day-in-the-life videos

Unscripted snapshots of our actual routines — mornings, therapies, school runs, wind-downs — so you can see strategies in motion, not just in theory.

02

Practical guides

Step-by-step playbooks for the big stuff: diagnosis day, IEP meetings, picky eating, sleep, public meltdowns, and telling family what you need.

03

Printable resources

Visual schedules, sensory checklists, appointment trackers, and scripts you can download, print, and stick on the fridge tonight.

04

A community that gets it

Comment sections that feel like group chats. Thousands of parents trading wins, warnings, and encouragement — no judgment, ever.
